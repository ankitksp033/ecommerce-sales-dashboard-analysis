# Business Insights

## 1. Discount Impact Analysis
Higher discount levels increased sales volume but reduced profitability margins, indicating aggressive discounting pressure.

---

## 2. Regional Performance
The western region generated the strongest sales contribution across all regions.

---

## 3. Seasonal Trends
Sales peaked during year-end months, suggesting strong seasonal purchasing behavior.

---

## 4. Product Profitability
Some high-selling products delivered lower profit margins, indicating pricing inefficiencies.

---

## 5. Customer Segments
Corporate and Consumer segments contributed the majority of overall revenue.
